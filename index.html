01 · The brand

A body scrub brand with a message

You don't need to become somebody else.

The scrub

Dessert-scented brown sugar body polish. Thick, glossy and made to be filmed.

The can

A clear can instead of the usual jar, so it stands out next to every other scrub on Takealot.

The details

Small hidden self-love reminders on the lid, seal and card. That's what people remember.

Brand lines
  • Don't compare. Just glow.
  • Made for the skin you're in.
  • Softer skin. Happier you.
  • A sweeter you.
  • Glow at your pace.
  • A little self-love goes a long way.
How it feels

Feminine and premium, but not overly girly or childish. Dessert and bakery imagery mixed with clean beauty packaging. The message is never shouted. The customer discovers it in the small details.

Why it can work: dessert scents are what's selling in body scrubs right now, and on Takealot most scrubs look the same: a wide jar and a plain label. A clear can with a caramel texture inside looks different in a tiny search-result photo.

03 · Packaging

The clever part

Not the short, wide jar every scrub uses. A clear, can-shaped container, so the caramel texture becomes the design.

Container: clear can shape, about 9.2cm tall with a 5.5cm opening. The label doesn't cover everything, so you see the polish.

Two lids: a sealed, tamper-evident lid for hygiene and delivery, plus a separate reusable lid inside (champagne gold if affordable, cream if plastic is much cheaper). Branded on top so it still looks designed from above in a bathroom.

Open→reveal→replace lid→keep using

Box: a cream or blush window box for gifts and online orders, closed with a sticker seal.

Small details to discover
  • Seal sticker: "Don't compare. Just glow. ♡"
  • Card inside: "Post your scrub moment ♡ #AmoraGlow. Tag us for a chance to be featured."
  • A different tiny message per batch
  • Numbered stickers on limited drops
Messages for batches
Softer skin. Happier you.A sweeter you.Glow at your pace.Made for the skin you're in.

Cost rule: same container, same lid, same label size. Only the artwork changes per scent. That's how it becomes collectible without costing more.

More ways to save
  • Buy sugar and sunflower oil at the supermarket on special. They're the same as the Takealot ones.
  • Use clean jam jars from home for testing. The real cans are only for selling.
  • Use a kitchen scale, bowl and spoon you already have. No gloves needed for testing, just clean hands.
  • Buy a 10ml fragrance first. Only buy the big bottle once you love the smell.
  • Later, for a "luxe" version, swap in sweet almond oil. Keep the cheap recipe as your main product.
05 · Prove it first

Find out people want it before you buy stock

The cheapest mistake is the one you catch before spending. Successful founders test the idea with real people, then set a clear "go" number.

1 · Real feedback

Give 5 to 10 people a test jar. Ask how it smells, how it feels after the shower, what they'd pay, and whether they'd buy it again. Write the answers down word for word.

2 · Polls and waitlist

Show the texture on Instagram Stories. Run polls on the scent and the can. Ask people to reply "ME" or join a WhatsApp list to be first when it launches.

3 · Pre-orders

The strongest proof is people paying before it exists. Open a small pre-order for the first 30 cans at your launch price.

Your "go" rule

If 20 people join the waitlist or pre-order within 2 weeks, make the first 30 cans and list on Takealot. If not, change one thing (scent, price or photos) and test again.

07 · Takealot

Takealot is the main shop

South Africa's biggest online store. Takealot stores, packs and delivers for you, so you only make and send stock. The work is winning the search result and getting the first reviews.

What you need to sign up
  • Your ID and a bank account. You don't need a registered company.
  • A barcode (EAN) for every product. Each scent and each set needs its own. You buy these from a barcode supplier.
  • Product details: name, size, ingredients, a short description.
  • Apply on the Takealot Seller Portal and wait for approval.
Photos that win the click
  • Main photo: the can on a white background, filling most of the frame, at least 1000 × 1000 pixels.
  • 5 to 7 photos in total. Add the texture close-up, the polish on skin, the lid, the size in a hand, and the box.
  • Your can is the advantage here: in a grid of wide jars, a clear caramel can stands out.
How stock works

Stock at Takealot (recommended): you send cans to their warehouse and they deliver fast. Fast delivery also helps you show up first.

Leadtime: you keep stock at home and send each order to Takealot within about 4 to 5 working days. Less risk, slower delivery.

Start with Leadtime while you test, then move to stock at Takealot once it sells.

Getting the first sales
  • Start a little lower while you have no reviews, then raise the price.
  • Ask friends and family to buy through Takealot and leave honest reviews.
  • Put a card in the box: "Loved it? Tell others in a Takealot review ♡". Never offer anything in return for a review.
  • Send every Instagram post and Reel to your Takealot link.
  • Try a small Sponsored Ads budget once you have a few reviews.
  • Watch the Promotions Calendar for Daily Deals and the Blue Dot Sale (Black Friday is 27 November 2026).

11 · Business setup

Make it official, step by step

You don't need to do all of this on day one. Start simple and add as sales grow.

Money help: NYDA grant

If you're 18 to 35 and a South African citizen, the National Youth Development Agency gives grants that don't need to be paid back, plus mentoring and business training. You apply online, do a business training course and give a 10-minute pitch. Processing takes about 30 working days.

This plan, your numbers and your pitch below are most of what you'd need.

Your pitch, in your words

Amora is a South African beauty brand creating dessert-inspired, sensorial self-care products designed to make everyday beauty feel like a little ritual. Its first hero product, Crème Brûlée Body Polish, combines a warm brown-sugar texture with distinctive clear can-style packaging, a reusable lid and small hidden self-love messages. Amora makes inexpensive packaging memorable through clever details, collectible seasonal artwork, limited numbered drops and highly shareable textures, all centred on one idea: Don't compare. Just glow.

12 · Risks and plan B

What could go wrong, and what you'd do

If this happensPlan B
Takealot takes a long time to approve youSell on Instagram, WhatsApp and Amazon.co.za meanwhile. Keep collecting reviews and photos.
Oil separates or floats on topAdd a little more sugar or coconut oil. Test in a warm room before you make a big batch.
The vanilla turns the scrub darkerNormal with vanilla. Brown sugar hides it. Mention "colour may deepen" on the label.
Sales are slow in the first monthChange one thing at a time: main photo, price, then title. Check your weekly numbers to see what moved.
Someone has a skin reactionRefund kindly, ask what happened, check the fragrance level. Keep your batch records so you can trace it.
Bad review on TakealotReply politely and fix the cause. A few honest reviews of any kind are better than none.
A bigger brand copies the can ideaYour name, messages, drops and community are harder to copy. Register the Amora trademark.
